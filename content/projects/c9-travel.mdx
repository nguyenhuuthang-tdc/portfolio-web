## Project overview

**Role:** Fullstack Developer  
**Client:** Corporate client  
**Period:** August 2024 – October 2024  
**Team size:** 4

Developed a multilingual content management system that lets businesses control customer-facing website content through an admin dashboard and APIs. Tours, fixtures, and tournaments can be updated centrally and delivered to customers quickly.

## Responsibilities

- Built dynamic multilingual CMS interfaces with Next.js and TypeScript.
- Developed and optimized Node.js REST APIs with Express.js.
- Applied Incremental Static Regeneration and server-side caching to publish tour schedule updates without a full page reload.
