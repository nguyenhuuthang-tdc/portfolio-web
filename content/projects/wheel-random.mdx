## Overview

WheelRandom is an advanced prize-wheel platform built for campaigns that need stronger transparency and auditability than a conventional random wheel.

It supports two draw modes: a standard mode for everyday use and a proof mode for high-trust events. In proof mode, the entries, winner, and draw information are recorded through a smart contract on BNB Smart Chain and can be publicly inspected on BscScan. Once confirmed on-chain, the published evidence cannot be silently altered.

## Key features

- Standard and blockchain-backed proof draw modes.
- Publicly verifiable draw data, entries, and winners.
- Wallet connection for signing proof draws and paying network gas fees.
- Separate frontend and backend services for the wheel experience and application APIs.
