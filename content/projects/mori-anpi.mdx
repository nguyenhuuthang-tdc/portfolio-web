## Project overview

**Role:** Fullstack Developer  
**Client:** Corporate client (Japan)  
**Period:** March 2025 – July 2025  
**Team size:** 7

Developed and maintained a disaster damage reporting system for assets and individuals after natural disasters such as earthquakes and tsunamis in Japan. The system focused on synchronizing and consolidating data between multiple services for accurate, timely reporting.

## Responsibilities

- Architected PostgreSQL schemas and optimized indexes for high-concurrency disaster reports.
- Built synchronization workflows between Nuxt/Laravel services and legacy systems to maintain consistent data.
- Developed Laravel REST APIs for real-time, high-priority damage and emergency reports.
